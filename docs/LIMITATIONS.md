# Limitations of v0

These were measured during the investigation that led to v1. They are the reasons v0 is a baseline rather than a usable component.

## It learns the generator, not real errors
- Training pairs come only from hand-written rules. Every improvement required adding a rule, and error types without a rule (most OCR errors, many real typing habits) are never learned.
- With the original data (random typos only), 93% of the character errors in 27 real sentences belonged to categories the generator almost never produced. On those sentences the 3-epoch model made the text slightly *worse* (CER 6.79% → 7.12%).
- Typo volume matters. Lowering it to 0.39× of the original data cut single-typo fixes at the same training step, and multi-error words stay hard even for the fully trained model: 30% of words with three typos are restored.

## It rewrites everything, with no confidence
- The decoder regenerates the whole sentence. There is no notion of confidence and no way to keep only safe edits.
- On already-correct sentences, the 3-epoch model changed about 1 word in 30 and altered 1 sentence in 4. In a pipeline that corrupts names and terms retrieval depends on.

## The input is narrow
- The vocabulary is 36 letters plus space. Punctuation, digits, Latin letters and diacritics never reach the model. `inference.py` passes them through around Arabic spans, but the model cannot use them as context.
- Training sentences are 15–128 characters with no punctuation or numbers, unlike real documents.
- One chunk is at most 128 characters. Longer text is split at word boundaries, and context does not cross chunks.

## Architecture quirks (inherited from the AraSpell design)
- The feed-forward blocks have no activation function, so each is a single linear map.
- Attention output is concatenated with the input instead of using the standard projection.
- About half of the positional-encoding dimensions barely change across positions.
- Checkpoints trained before padding masks were added (`mask_padding` missing from the checkpoint) attend to padding positions. Their input must be padded exactly as in training (141 tokens), which `inference.py` does. Without it, character error rate rose from 4.06% to 6.78%.

## Speed and packaging
- Greedy decoding recomputes the decoder at every step: about 6 sentences per second on an RTX 3060.
- The custom architecture and 40-token vocabulary cannot be loaded as a standard Hugging Face model.

## Evaluation
- The test set is generated by the same rules as the training data, and it doubles as the validation set. Results on it say how well the model undoes its own noise, not how it performs on real text.
- The only real-text check is a 27-sentence benchmark.

v1 addresses these with per-character edit labels, a full character set, pretraining, real OCR and real-edit data, and frozen real test sets; see the `v1` branch.
